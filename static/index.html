One score, built in three steps.

Ask three times

Your question goes to GPT-4o-mini three separate times at temperature 0.9. Different wording is fine. Different facts are a warning sign.

Three judges vote

Qwen3 27B, Mistral Medium 3.5 and Llama 3.3 70B each decide, without seeing each other, whether every answer is correct or hallucinated. Each one reads fresh web search results first.

Count what agrees

Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite checks whether the three answers reach the same conclusion. Each judge and the agreement check count equally. Nemotron then double-checks the best answer and takes 10 points off if it disagrees with the panel.

Questions people ask.

Does a high score mean the answer is true?

No. It means the judges and the three answers agree. On TruthfulQA the models often share the same myth, so a wrong answer can still score 100.

Which models are used?

GPT-4o-mini writes the three answers. Qwen3 27B, Mistral Medium 3.5 and Llama 3.3 70B judge each one using Tavily web search results, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite checks whether the answers reach the same conclusion, and Nemotron 3.5 Lightning double-checks the winner.

What do the confidence labels mean?

A score of 80 or more is high confidence, 75 to 79 is medium, and anything below 75 is low. Treat medium and low scores as a reason to check another source.

What if a model fails to respond?

The score is built from the judges that did respond, and failed attempts are listed in the result. If all three answers fail, the score is 0 with an error message.

What kind of question works best?

Factual questions with one checkable answer, such as dates, places and names. It is weakest on common misconceptions and opinions.
